# Team Agreement

**Team name:** „Keturių lapų klevas“

**Members:** Lukas Stavaris, Erwin Gasperowicz, Emilis Juknevičius, Tomas Čivilis, Andrius Šilinskas

**Team leader:** Tomas Čivilis — chosen by voting, the easiest and quickest way to make the decision.

## Team Leader's Authority

- Scheduling authority — set meeting times, send reminders
- Check on the team members' work status
- Submit assignments
- Tie-breaker in conflicts

## Team Ambition

Grade — 9

## Key Team Rules

- Inform each other about AI usage
- Do not be late
- Be accountable
- Respond to team messages within one workday
- Do not use ambiguous emojis when communicating in chat
- Disagreements get resolved within 48h of being raised; if not, resolve through the team leader
- Update status on GitHub when working on a ticket

## Key Principles Governing Work Distribution

- Divide work based on the members' capacity
- Choose tasks based on interest

## Capacity Constraints

Up to 5 hours per member.

## Team Meeting Time

Meet on Wednesday at 20:00, when necessary.

## Progress Tracking Method

GitHub Issues feature.

## Sanctions and Punishment

We give constructive criticism, not punishments. If that does not work, contact the teacher.

## AI Usage Policy

We will not use AI for code generation. It can be used for research, debugging, documentation, and checking text for mistakes.
